How auto-continue works
Google lets a normal Gmail account send about {{ daily_limit }} emails per rolling 24 hours. With auto-continue on, a check runs every minute and sends the waiting people, always stopping about 50 short of the limit so your own mail keeps working. As earlier sends pass 24 hours, it carries on by itself.
  1. One-time setup: in Render, open your service, then Environment, and add CRON_SECRET with any long random text.
  2. On cron-job.org, create a job with the address https://{{ request.get_host }}/cron/continue/?key=YOUR_CRON_SECRET that runs every 1 minute.
  3. While your Google app is in Testing mode, your sign-in expires after 7 days. If the status says so, log in again.
